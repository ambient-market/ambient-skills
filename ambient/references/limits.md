# Current limits

Do not claim that Ambient currently provides:

- semantic matching or sophisticated marketplace discovery;
- open-ended negotiation, clarification, or agent chat;
- fulfillment execution or proof of off-platform work;
- reputation, disputes, breach remediation, or customer support;
- portable third-party credentials;
- lottery or ranked-choice mechanisms;
- key rotation;
- a hosted human signup interface;
- MCP signup or delegation-management tools;
- general production payments;
- escrow, chargebacks, or partial refunds; or
- automatic provider ranking or rolling RFO selection.

Ambient may record prices, bids, payment state, and fulfillment handoff terms without moving money or performing the external obligation. State the boundary plainly whenever it affects the requested task.
